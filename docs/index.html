The architectural contribution.

A harness is the workflow, checks and instructions around an agent. This design gives evidence and active rules separate lifecycles, joining them through explicit approval and recorded failures. Evidence stays available when an intervention needs revision.

Plain rules

An instruction file tells an agent what to do. The loop also keeps the evidence, scope and decision available for later review.

Retrieval and authority

Retrieval can surface a past event. A candidate is not active guidance until a person makes and records the policy decision.

Reflection and correction

Automatic reflection can suggest an action. Here, later recorded violations can prompt a person to sharpen, enforce or retire it.

Small enough to inspect.

The standard-library CLI reads a supplied ledger. load() bounds and parses the input; validate() checks each entry; main() checks or renders active rules.

An excerpt match is a string check; approval is an attestation, not an authenticated identity. The checker does not establish source truth or semantic support, automatically activate rules, change model weights or enforce guidance in a host.

Designed to be revised.

A palinode is a poem that revises an earlier position. Here the name is a reminder that rules, too, are provisional. Corrigibility means keeping the decision open to correction: preserve the evidence, inspect the result, and let a person change course.
