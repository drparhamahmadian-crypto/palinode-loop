`docs/assets/fraunces.ttf` is Fraunces, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its unmodified copyright and licence notice is included at [`docs/assets/OFL.txt`](docs/assets/OFL.txt). The font's licence applies separately from the repository's MIT licence.

Source: [Google Fonts, Fraunces directory](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/6c1dbe27dcf7d817d4b62cec033ba77fa8e2a5ec/ofl/fraunces). Retrieved 3 November 2026 from pinned commit `6c1dbe27dcf7d817d4b62cec033ba77fa8e2a5ec`; the original font filename is `Fraunces[SOFT,WONK,opsz,wght].ttf`. Only its local asset filename was shortened; its font data is unmodified.

The explainer loads the bundled font locally. It does not contact Google Fonts or a content delivery network.
